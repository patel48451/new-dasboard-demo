Assumptions: Ferrotech is a modeled MRO parts distributor used to demonstrate the tool — swap in your real SKUs in the table above; every input cell recalculates live. Safety stock uses SS = z × σ_d × √L, where σ_d is estimated from an editable demand coefficient-of-variation (CV%) per item rather than raw order history. Under Periodic Review, the lead-time term extends to (L + review period) and the ROP column becomes an Order-up-to Level (S). EOQ is shown as a cost-minimizing reference quantity in both policies, per standard practice. ABC classes use the classic 80/95 cumulative-value cutoffs.
